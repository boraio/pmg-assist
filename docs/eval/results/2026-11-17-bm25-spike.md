# BM25 spike over gold 0.1, 2026-11-17

A throwaway check, run before the evaluation harness was written, that DuckDB
full-text search works over the gold chunks and is fast enough. It is **not**
Milestone 1's Recall@5: the set had 13 questions, all drafted from records
sampled for the purpose.

## Setup

- Chunks: `data/gold/committee-question/index=0.1/chunks.parquet`, 149,067
  chunks over 41,155 records (ADR 0004).
- Search: DuckDB 1.5.5 `fts` with its default settings, over each chunk's
  `searched` text (context line plus chunk text).
- A record's rank is the rank of its best chunk; the top 20 records were kept.
- Questions: `docs/eval/committee-question.yaml`, q001–q013, all answerable.

Index build: 17.9 s. Each question: 0.07–0.15 s.

Recall@5 is 11 of 13 (0.85) and MRR is 0.67. Both were worked out from the
ranks above; the script's own summary line did not print.

## Why the number flatters keyword search

The questions were drafted while reading their records, and most carry a
distinctive name straight from the record: SAFCOL, Tonosa, Dunoon, NPA,
KwaZulu-Natal. BM25 is strong on exactly those. Names matter for this app,
which is why ADR 0004 keeps BM25, but a set made mostly of them overstates
it. Later questions are to be written in everyday words, as someone who has not
read the record would ask.

## The two misses

- **q009, "overcrowded public schools": not in the top 20.** The record
  (pmg-committee-question-32760) has 4 chunks; none contains "overcrowd" and
  all 4 contain "ratio" (learner-to-school and learner-to-educator ratios). The
  citizen's word and the record's words do not overlap, which keyword search
  cannot bridge. This is the case vector search is meant for.
- **q012, black farmers supported under the AAMP: rank 13.** The 12 records
  above it are mostly other agriculture and farmer-support questions (Land Bank
  and AgriBEE, black farmers' exports, AAMP growth projections, farmers under
  pressure, agricultural production, funding plans), plus two about rural
  municipalities and agribusiness, and one about sector education. They share
  the question's words, so the right record is crowded out. The nearest of
  them, at rank 5 (pmg-committee-question-19131, 2022), asks about the AAMP
  itself, but its reply says no details exist yet because the plan had only
  just been signed, so it does not answer q012.

## What this settles

- DuckDB `fts` works over the gold chunks with DuckDB 1.5.5.
- Building the index takes under 20 seconds, so the harness can rebuild it on
  every run.
- Paraphrase (q009) and crowding by shared words (q012) are the two failure
  modes to watch when vectors and fusion are added.
